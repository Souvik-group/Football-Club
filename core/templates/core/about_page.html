WE ARE

AGNI

Arjungeria AGNI Sangha is a passionate football club dedicated to promoting sportsmanship, teamwork, excellence in football. Our club has been a center for young talent and community spirit.

We organize regular matches, training sessions, and tournaments to develop players and encourage football culture in our area. Our mission is to build a striving football community and inspire the next generation of players.
